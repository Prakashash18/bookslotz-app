BookSlot for educators · UI mockups

One lecturer, one link, a whole class booked.

BookSlot is for educators with one problem: a room full of students who each need a time with you. You open a window, the app cuts it into slots, and the class books itself — no scheduling email thread. This is a working flow, not static frames: slots are generated for real from the availability and duration you set, the cap is applied, disabled slots drop out, and each booking removes that time for everyone else. Desktop and mobile share one state, so a click in either updates both.
